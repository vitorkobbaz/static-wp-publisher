# Commercial model

The Core is distributed through WordPress.org without page or build quotas. Portable export exists only in a separately distributed add-on.

Initial annual reference pricing:

- Individual: USD 59 / one WordPress installation.
- Agency: USD 149 / 25 installations.
- Multisite: USD 199 / one network.
- Unlimited: USD 299 / unlimited legitimate client installations and networks.

Freemius is the initial merchant and licensing provider. A seven-day card-required trial renews automatically after reminders. Official updates and support expire with annual subscriptions; the installed paid version continues to work. The Core never depends on Freemius.

Commercial terms, privacy, trial, billing, trademark, and lifetime-license pricing require legal review before sale.
